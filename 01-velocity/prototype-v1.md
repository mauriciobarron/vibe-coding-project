## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** The product provides a clear, at-a-glance view of the entire sales pipeline.
- **What confused them:** The prototype makes the pipeline data clear, but it is less clear how users would actively use the tool to manage their day-to-day work.
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** That a CRM designed around how sales representatives actually work would improve adoption compared with a traditional CRM.
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** One of the main open questions is which metric matters most and what action the user should take next. The prototype successfully makes the pipeline visible across its stages and provides an immediate view of the portfolio, but it does not clearly guide the user on what to do with that information. The CRM needs stronger calls to action and action-oriented features that turn insight into next steps.
